Common questions

Does GTA 6 have a 60fps mode?

No. Every version targets 30fps, including the PS5 Pro. There's no 40fps or 60fps option.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not yet. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both came to PC more than a year after console.

Will the physical copy save me downloading?

No. Rockstar has confirmed physical copies contain a download code, not a disc, so you download the full game either way.

Don't have the game yet?

Grand Theft Auto VI for PS5, sold and shipped by Amazon AU. The box contains a download code, not a disc, so you'll still download the full game.
