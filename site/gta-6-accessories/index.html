02 · Controllers

DualSense or DualSense Edge?

Get a second standard DualSense. GTA 6 is a long game, and swapping to a charged controller beats playing on the cable. A spare is also handy for anyone else in the house.

The DualSense Edge is a pro controller. Back buttons, swappable sticks and adjustable triggers help in competitive shooters. In a 30fps open-world game they make little difference, and owners regularly report shorter battery life than the standard pad. Buy it for other games if you want it, not for GTA 6.

Can I use any headphones with a PS5?

Yes. Wired headphones plug into the 3.5mm jack on the DualSense. Sony's own wireless headsets connect directly. Most Bluetooth headphones don't work with PS5 without an adapter.

Do I need a headset on Xbox?

No, but if you want one, get an Xbox-specific wireless model. PlayStation headsets like the Pulse Elite won't connect to an Xbox.

Is a soundbar better than a headset?

For playing alone at night, a headset is better and cheaper. For a shared lounge room, get a soundbar.
