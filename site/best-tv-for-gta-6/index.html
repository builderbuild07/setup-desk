Pick by room

Sunny lounge room or dark one?

This matters more than any spec sheet. Most Aussie lounge rooms get a lot of daylight, and an OLED that looks perfect at night can look dim at 2pm with the blinds open.

Sunny room, or you mostly play during the day: get mini-LED. The TCL C7 or Hisense U7 will be brighter than any OLED at this price.

Darker room, or you mostly play at night: get OLED. Perfect blacks make night drives and neon-lit streets look far better. The LG C5 is the pick; the B5/B6 if money's tight.

Size: most Aussie lounge rooms suit 55 to 65 inches. If you sit more than about 2.5 metres away, go 65.

Do I need a 120Hz TV for GTA 6?

Not for GTA 6 at launch, since it runs at 30fps. It's still worth having for other games and in case a higher frame-rate mode arrives later.

Which HDMI port should I use?

An HDMI 2.1 port, usually labelled 4K@120 or eARC. Use the cable that came with the console.

What picture mode should I use?

Game mode with HDR on, and motion smoothing off. Avoid Vivid. Our settings checklist walks through it.
