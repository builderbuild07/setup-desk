The best TV for GTA 6

GTA 6 runs at 30fps, so 120Hz and VRR matter less here than in most new games. What you'll notice is HDR brightness, contrast and a sharp 4K panel. Here are the exact models and sizes to buy for your room.

Before you choose

Sunny lounge room or dark one?

This matters more than any spec sheet. Most Aussie lounge rooms get a lot of daylight, and an OLED that looks perfect at night can look dim at 2pm with the blinds open.

Sunny room, or you mostly play during the day: get mini-LED. The Hisense U7S or TCL C7L will be brighter than any OLED at the price.

Darker room, or you mostly play at night: get OLED. Perfect blacks make night drives and neon-lit streets look far better. The LG C6 is the one to get.

Size: most Aussie lounge rooms suit 55 to 65 inches. My rule of thumb: if you sit more than about 2.5 metres away, go 65.

Do I need a 120Hz TV for GTA 6?

Not for GTA 6, which runs at 30fps on every console. It's still worth having for other games.

Should I buy last year's LG C5 instead?

Only if it's clearly cheaper than the C6. Stores are clearing out the C5, and C6 prices have already dropped, so the gap is often small.

Which HDMI port should I use?

An HDMI 2.1 port, usually labelled 4K@120 or eARC. Use the cable that came with the console.

What picture mode should I use?

Game mode with HDR on, and motion smoothing off. Avoid Vivid. Our settings checklist walks through it.
