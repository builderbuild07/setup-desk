The best console for GTA 6 in Australia

Every console runs GTA 6 at 30fps. What you're paying for is a sharper picture and fewer frame-rate drops. Here's how the four compare, and which one to buy.

The big question

Is the PS5 Pro worth $400 more?

If you're buying a console mainly for GTA 6 and have a 4K TV: yes. It won't give you 60fps, but it's the only console holding a near-locked 30, and PSSR makes the image visibly sharper on a big screen. The 2TB of built-in storage also helps with a 210GB game.

If you already own a PS5 or Series X: probably not. You're playing the same game at the same internal resolution and frame-rate target. Put the money toward a better TV or more storage instead.

If your TV is 1080p: the Pro's sharper image is mostly wasted. Buy a PS5 Digital Edition and save the difference for a 4K TV later.

Does GTA 6 have a 60fps mode?

No. Every version targets 30fps, including the PS5 Pro. There's no 40fps or 60fps option.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not yet. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date. GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 both came to PC more than a year after console.

Is it playable on Xbox Series S?

Yes, but it's the weakest version: 720p upscaled to about 1080p, and often below 30fps. If you're choosing a console for GTA 6, get a PS5.

Do I need the disc version of the PS5?

Not for GTA 6. Physical copies contain a download code, not a disc. You only need the disc drive for other games or Blu-rays.
