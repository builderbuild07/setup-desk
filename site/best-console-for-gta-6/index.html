The big question

Is the PS5 Pro worth $400 more?

If you're buying a console mainly for GTA 6 and have a 4K TV: yes. It won't give you 60fps, but it's the only console holding a near-locked 30, and PSSR makes the image visibly sharper on a big screen. The 2TB of built-in storage also helps with a 210GB game.

If you already own a PS5 or Series X: probably not. You're playing the same game at the same resolution and frame-rate target. Put the money toward a better TV or more storage instead.

If your TV is 1080p: the Pro's sharper image is mostly wasted. Buy a standard PS5 and save the difference for a 4K TV later.

Does GTA 6 have a 60fps mode?

Not at launch. Rockstar targets 30fps on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Series S. Rockstar has hinted more optimisation could come after release, so a patch is possible but not promised.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not yet. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date. Its past games have come to PC a year or more after console.

Is it playable on Xbox Series S?

Yes, but it's the weakest version: 720p upscaled to about 1080p, with frequent drops below 30fps. If you're choosing a console for GTA 6, go for a PS5 or Series X.

Can I play through Xbox Cloud Gaming?

Yes, but cloud streams the Series S version, so you get Series S image quality plus any lag from your internet connection.
