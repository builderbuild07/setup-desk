About Setup Desk

A small, independent guide to getting GTA 6 running well in Australia: what to buy, what to skip, and which settings to change.

By TomLast updated 20 November 2026

Who runs this

Setup Desk is written by Tom, a gamer based in Australia. Most setup guides are written for American prices and American lounge rooms. This one isn't.

Everything here is aimed at one question: if you're about to play GTA 6, what's worth spending money on, and what isn't? Sometimes the answer is "nothing, just change these settings".

Where the information comes from

  • Performance numbers (resolution, frame rates) come from Digital Foundry's launch analysis (November 2026). We say so wherever we use them.
  • Prices are Australian RRPs or typical prices at major Australian stores, with the date we checked them. For anything that changes weekly, we link to the live price instead.
  • Unconfirmed details, like the download size, are marked "reportedly" until they're confirmed.
  • The money tracker only lists methods we've timed ourselves in-game.

How the site is paid for

Through Amazon affiliate links and Google ads. Neither one decides our verdicts. The affiliate disclosure has the details.

Get in touch

Spotted a mistake, or a price that's changed? Email you@example.com. Corrections get fixed and the "last updated" date changes.
