What I'd do first

If you only do two things, turn on Game mode and turn off motion smoothing on your TV. Those fix most of what looks laggy or smeary. Then run your console's HDR calibration before you start the game.

Why bother

Why these settings matter more at 30fps

At 60fps, a bit of extra input lag or a smeary picture mode is easy to miss. At 30fps it adds up. Game mode and turning off motion smoothing take the most lag out, and proper HDR calibration is what makes the night city look right instead of grey.

If your TV is older and doesn't have HDMI 2.1, don't worry. GTA 6 at 30fps doesn't need the extra bandwidth. Just make sure Game mode is on.
