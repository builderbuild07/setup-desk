How we choose what to recommend

  • We recommend products based on performance data (like Digital Foundry's launch testing), what we've used ourselves, and value for Australian buyers.

  • We tell you what to skip as well as what to buy, even when skipping it means we earn nothing.
  • Commission rates don't decide anything. Our pick is our pick whether Amazon pays 1% or 10% on it.
