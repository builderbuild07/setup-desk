Who we are

Setup Desk is an independent website about setting up GTA 6, run from Australia by Tom. "We", "us" and "our" in this policy mean Setup Desk.

We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Not much. There are no accounts, sign-up forms or comments on this site, and we don't ask for your name or email.

  • Settings checklist: when you tick boxes on the checklist page, your ticks are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we use your address only to reply.
  • Analytics: we don't currently use an analytics service. If we add one, we'll update this page first.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to show ads on some pages. Google and its partners are third-party vendors, and they use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Third-party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads here and use cookies to do so.

You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tools.

Amazon affiliate links

Setup Desk is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program (Amazon Australia). When you click an Amazon link on this site, Amazon may set a cookie so it can tell that you came from us and credit us with a commission if you buy something. Amazon handles that information under its own privacy notice.

We don't receive your name, address or what you bought. Amazon only reports totals to us.

Hosting

This site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify's servers process your IP address and basic request information to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

Your choices

  • Opt out of personalised advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Clear your checklist progress by tapping Reset on the checklist page, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Questions or requests about your information: email you@example.com.
