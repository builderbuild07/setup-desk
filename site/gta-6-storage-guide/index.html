Ten minutes, one screwdriver

How to fit an SSD in a PS5

  1. Update the console first

    Settings › System › System Software. Then turn the PS5 fully off (not rest mode) and unplug it.

  2. Take off the cover

    How it comes off depends on your model. Sony's manual for your console shows the exact steps.

  3. Fit the drive

    Remove the slot cover screw, slide the SSD in at an angle, press it flat and screw it down. Put the slot cover back on.

  4. Format it

    Put the cover back, plug in and turn on. The PS5 asks to format the new drive. Say yes.

  5. Install GTA 6 to it

    Settings › Storage › Installation Location, and set PS5 games to the M.2 drive. Or move existing games there to free up the internal drive.

On Xbox?

Xbox Series X and S can't use a normal SSD. You need an official Storage Expansion Card, which plugs into the back. No screwdriver needed.

Will the physical copy save me downloading?

No. Rockstar has confirmed physical copies contain a download code, not a disc, so you'll download the full game either way.

Can I play GTA 6 from a USB hard drive?

No. You can store it there to save re-downloading later, but PS5 and Xbox Series games have to be on the internal drive, an M.2 SSD (PS5) or an Expansion Card (Xbox) to play.

Do I really need the heatsink version?

Sony says an M.2 drive in the PS5 needs a heatsink or other cooling. The heatsink versions are made to fit, so buy one and you don't have to think about it.
