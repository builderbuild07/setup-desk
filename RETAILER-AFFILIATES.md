# Retailer affiliate programs (Australia)

_Researched 21 November 2026 by web search. Nothing has been signed up for._

Why this matters: Amazon AU doesn't sell most of the TVs, consoles and SSDs the site recommends (see CLAUDE.md), so those pages earn nothing yet. A local retailer program would let the "Try JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys…" lines become real links.

**How sure each fact is:**
- **Confirmed** means I read it on the retailer's own affiliate page.
- **Unconfirmed** means it only comes from third-party directories or affiliate-network listings. Those often disagree with each other and can be years out of date. Check the live terms after you sign up.

## The Good Guys

- **Program:** yes. **Confirmed** on [thegoodguys.com.au/affiliates](https://www.thegoodguys.com.au/affiliates).
- **Network:** Partnerize.
- **Commission:** 3% default.
- **Cookie:** 14 days.
- **Excluded brands:** Apple, **PlayStation 5, Xbox**, Asko, Miele, Loewe and WeByLoewe. Gift cards, Gold Service Extras and Home Services are excluded too.
  - So console links to The Good Guys would earn nothing.
  - TV, soundbar and headset links should earn the 3%.
- **How sure:** I could only read part of that page directly. The network, rate, cookie length and exclusions came from an automated summary of the rest. Confirm them on the page before relying on them.
- **Eligibility:** not stated in what I could read. You apply through Partnerize, and The Good Guys' affiliate team reviews the application.
- **Sign up:** [join.partnerize.com/thegoodguys/en](https://join.partnerize.com/thegoodguys/en)
- **Other listings:**
  - Skimlinks and Sovrn Commerce (formerly VigLink) also list the store. **Unconfirmed** whether those pay out or at what rate.
  - Older directories mention Commission Factory and Impact. These are probably out of date.
- **Best fit for:** the TV page, the soundbar and headset picks, and the bundles' TV items.

## Bing Lee

- **Program:** probably yes. **Unconfirmed:** Bing Lee's own site blocked automated reading, and the Commission Factory listing page returned "not found" when read directly.
- **Network:** Commission Factory, according to search results and several directories. Also listed on Sovrn Commerce and FlexOffers.
- **Commission:** listings disagree.
  - Commission Factory snippet: up to 2.5%, 1.5% for coupon sites, 0% on some brands (Apple, Dyson, Miele named), 30-day tracking.
  - Wealthy Affiliate: up to 3%.
  - FlexOffers: 0.8–2%.
  - **Unconfirmed** whether consoles are excluded, as they are at The Good Guys.
- **Can a small site join?**
  - Joining Commission Factory as a publisher is free. You then apply to each brand's program separately, and the brand approves or declines.
  - No minimum traffic is published for Bing Lee. **Unconfirmed.**
- **Sign up:**
  - Commission Factory publisher sign-up: [commissionfactory.com/affiliates](https://www.commissionfactory.com/affiliates)
  - Then search for "Bing Lee" in the advertiser directory once you're in.
- **Best fit for:** TVs (the TV page already cites Bing Lee prices).

## Mwave

- **Program:** yes. **Confirmed** on [mwave.com.au/pages/affiliate](https://www.mwave.com.au/pages/affiliate).
- **Joining:** free. Mwave's page says "The MWAVE.COM.AU Affiliate Program costs $0 to join."
- **Who can apply:** "streamer, content creator, influencer, run/write blogs or sites". Mwave "reserves the right to deny or revoke any applications", and only successful applicants are contacted.
- **Commission:** the page only says "a commission (% of the sale)", with no figure and no cookie length.
  - Third-party listings say 1–2%. **Unconfirmed.** One listing claims 5–10%, which is very unlikely for computer parts.
- **Network:** not named on Mwave's page.
  - One tracker says it moved from Commission Factory to Impact. **Unconfirmed.**
  - Sovrn Commerce and Skimlinks-style listings also exist.
- **Sign up:** the "Apply Today" button on [mwave.com.au/pages/affiliate](https://www.mwave.com.au/pages/affiliate). I couldn't see where it leads.
- **Best fit for:** the SSDs on the Storage page (WD_BLACK SN850X, Samsung 990 PRO, Crucial P310). The page already names Mwave.

## JB Hi-Fi

- **Program:** **no direct program found.**
  - There's no affiliate page on jbhifi.com.au: the likely address returned "not found".
  - It isn't listed on Commission Factory, Partnerize or Impact as far as I could find.
- **Third-party listings:**
  - A third-party directory shows a "JB Hi-Fi AU" campaign on **Skimlinks** with "minimum 0% CPA". **Unconfirmed** whether it pays anything.
  - Other listings are gift-card or voucher offers from unrelated companies. Ignore those.
- **Other JB Hi-Fi programs:** in February 2026 JB Hi-Fi launched a retail media network with Criteo. That's for brands buying ads on JB's site, not for affiliates.
- **What to do:** if you join Skimlinks for other reasons, check whether JB Hi-Fi links earn there. Otherwise treat JB Hi-Fi as a plain, unpaid link.

## Harvey Norman

- **Program:** **no affiliate program found** in Australia.
  - Link-checking directories report none across 120+ networks.
  - Older forum posts say the same.
- **What does exist:**
  - **Customer Direct Partner Program:** for marketplace sellers, not affiliates.
  - **Commercial division:** trade buyers only.
  - **HN Club:** a customer loyalty scheme.
  - **Singapore program:** Harvey Norman Singapore once ran one through Involve Asia. That's a different company and no use here.
- **What to do:** keep it as a plain, unpaid store name.

## One shortcut worth knowing about

**Skimlinks** and **Sovrn Commerce** are "sub-networks". You join once, and they pay you on links to thousands of stores, The Good Guys, Mwave and possibly JB Hi-Fi among them. Their rates are lower than joining each program directly, because they take a cut.
- **Small sites:** both accept small sites, but each reviews your site first. **Unconfirmed** whether they'd approve a brand-new site with little traffic.
- **Amazon conflict:** don't run their automatic link-converting script on Amazon links. Amazon's rules require your own Associates tag on those.

## Suggested order

1. **The Good Guys (Partnerize).** It's the only one with a confirmed program, rate and sign-up page, and it covers the TVs. Remember consoles earn 0%.
2. **Commission Factory, then Bing Lee.** One sign-up also opens other Australian retailers.
3. **Mwave.** Covers the SSDs.
4. **Before adding any of these links to the site:**
   - Update the Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure to name the new network. CLAUDE.md requires this, and Partnerize, Commission Factory and Impact all set tracking cookies.
   - Add `rel="sponsored"` to the new links.
